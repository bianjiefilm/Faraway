About Faraway

Faraway is a focused productivity and eye-health utility for macOS. It helps users build consistent break habits with predictable local reminders based on the 20-20-20 rule.

What Faraway is

Faraway is designed as a lightweight app that runs in the menu bar and prompts users to look away for short breaks. The product philosophy is simple: reduce screen fatigue without adding complexity.

The mission is simple: remind users to rest their eyes regularly and protect attention from the hidden drag of long coding, editing, and AI-assisted sessions. We built Faraway as a free product to help more people stay healthier through predictable breaks. We keep improving it through regular releases and practical updates.

  • Local-first reminders on macOS 13+.
  • Configurable cadence and app-aware scheduling for focused workflows.
  • Public source of truth through GitHub releases, archive, and issue tracker.

Core use cases

  • Designers and developers working long coding or creative sessions.
  • Writers and analysts with frequent screen focus.
  • Teams standardizing micro-break habits in local Mac workflows.
